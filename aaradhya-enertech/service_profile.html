Overview
Established in 2012, Aaradhya Enertech Industries is an aspiring player in the repairing and manufacturing of distribution and power transformers. The company is registered with MSME and has the entire infrastructure to repair & manufacture Transformers up to 10MVA and voltage class of 11KV, 22KV, 33KV. The manufacturing is in accordance with the strict quality assurance procedure which meets all national standards.

We have ISO certified 9001-2015 and 14001-2015 & empanelment as a channel partner for grid-connected rooftop & Small solar power plants program of Ministry of new and renewable energy. We have enlisted as a vendor for supply APL & BPL Kit. Also empanelled as a turnkey contractor under state Discoms.
