LT Transformers Up to 1.1 KV

Three phase or single phase dry - type low voltage (LV) transformers are used as isolation transformers or to adjust the voltage level in the LV distribution grid. The windings are made of aluminium or copper and impregnated with epoxy resin of thermal class F or H by simple or vacuum pressure impregnation (VPI). ATS low voltage transformers are widely used in industrial applications and in commercial and public building. They are also used for adjusting the voltage from 690 V to 400 V or as isolation transformers with a voltage ratio of 1:1,providing galvanic separation.
