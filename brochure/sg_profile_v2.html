SG Enterprises Products & Range

Power Transformer (3.15 MVA to 5 MVA - 33 KV)

A power transformer is used primarily to couple electrical energy from a power supply line to a circuit system, or to one or more components of the system. A power transformer used with solid state circuits is called a rectifier transformer. The rating is given in terms of the secondary's maximum voltage and current-delivering capacity.

Power transformers above 5 MVA are used in Boards Substations, while ratings up to 6.3 MVA are used in various heavy load applications including Industry, Hospitals, Railways, Refineries, and Substations. These are custom-built for specific power distribution requirements.

Distribution Transformer (5 KVA to 2500 KVA)

A transformer transfers electrical energy from one circuit to another without changing the frequency. Transformers are used to meet a wide variety of needs by increasing or decreasing AC voltage. Up to 100 KVA transformers are mainly used in electricity boards & electrification of various government schemes. Other ratings are widely used in Industries, Hospitals, Colleges, Highways, and Refineries.

LT Transformers (Up to 1.1 KV)

Three-phase or single-phase dry-type low voltage (LV) transformers are used as isolation transformers or to adjust voltage levels in the LV distribution grid. Windings are made of aluminium or copper and impregnated with epoxy resin via VPI.

Dry Type Transformer (100 KVA to 2.5 MVA) - up to 33 KV

Cast resin dry type transformer (CRT) is used in high moisture prone areas. Its primary and secondary windings are encapsulated with epoxy resin. Complete protection is achieved by this best resin encapsulation so that the transformer can work without disruption in highly moisture prone areas. Thus this transformer is non hygroscopic. Available in ratings from 100 KVA to 2500 KVA with insulation class F.

Cast Resin Dry Type Transformer (CRT)

Better overload capacity. Low partial discharge along with low losses. Hence efficiency is very good. As it is with non inflammable winding insulation, it offers zero risk to fire hazard. So it is suitable for indoor installation. Can be fitted outdoor in IP 45 enclosure. And of course non hygroscopic.

The Manufacturing Stage Of Transformer

Core Assembly

The magnetic core of the transformer is built-up of cold rolled grain oriented (CRGO) steel lamination. Hi-B grade & laser scribed lamination is used to reduce the no load losses & noise level of transformer. Lamination are cut at an angle of 45 Deg, Core Leg & Core yoke laminations are interleaved in mitred joints in order to facilitate the passage of magnetic flux & to avoid hotspots & reduce No Load Losses, No Load current & noise level.

Coil Winding

The windings are designed to optimize dynamic, thermal, mechanical & electrical stresses depending upon the current & voltage requirements. Spiral crossover, Helical continuous disc, Fully or partly interleaved winding are used depending on the specific design criteria. The winding machines are equipped with hydraulic break system which ensures the proper tension is maintained during the winding. Dovetailed key spacers are employed to give the extra strength to the winding.

Core Coil Assembly

The magnetic core with the windings and some accessories fitted together forms the active part of the transformer. The windings are slipped over the core legs & laminations of the upper yoke are interleaved. All connections between windings and bushings & tappings to tap changer are made. The tap changer allows the increase or decrease of a certain no of turns in the high voltage winding in order to compensate for a voltage drop or to adopt the transformer to an unstable supply.

Drying/ Ovening

A transformer may contain no trace of humidity; otherwise there will be a danger of flash over within the windings. The active part of transformer is completely dried before transformer is filled with transformer oil under vacuum. The tank with active part in it, is connected to an oil supply and placed inside a vacuum chamber.

Tank/ Tanking

Our tank design is fully computerized. The computer determines the optimum size and the number and location of required tank supports. The objective is to reduce the tank size and weight as much as possible, which facilitates easier handling, transportation, assembly and installation on site. All metal parts are grit blast cleaned to remove weld splatter.

Surface Treatment & Painting

We use satisfactory protection against corrosion & rusting, all surfaces of steel parts are shot blasted in compliance with Swedish Standard SIS 055900 to Sa 2.5 quality prior to painting. Thereafter tanks and steel parts are painted with one primer and two final coats.

Testing

All transformers are subject to all routine tests in our sophisticated laboratory at the factory, confirming to the latest national and international standard specifications. Tests are intended for the basic validation of design & basis for performance, safety & reliability of the transformers. SG Enterprises is equipped with adequate digital precision meters such as the digital power analyser, WT-300 (latest) of Yokogawa of Japan make, which are used for measuring the load losses, impedance voltage, no load losses & no load currents.
